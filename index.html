RemixIT-TSE: Progressive Synthetic-to-Real Adaptation for
Target Speech Extraction via Target-Aware Supervision and Remixing

Yu Wang    Haixin Guan    Shuang Wei    Yanhua Long
Paper GitHub

Abstract

Target Speech Extraction (TSE) in real-world conversational scenarios suffers from severe performance degradation due to the domain gap between synthetic training data and complex acoustic environments, where signal-level ground truth is typically unavailable. To address this challenge, we make the first attempt to extend RemixIT from speech enhancement to TSE and propose a progressive synthetic-to-real adaptation framework for real-world TSE with two fine-tuning stages. The first stage leverages region-wise speaker similarity and silence constraints within a target-aware adaptation framework to jointly optimize the model using synthetic and weakly supervised real-world data, injecting real-world traits while preserving synthetic-learned capabilities. The second stage further adapts the model using only real-world data through our RemixIT-TSE, where quality-filtered teacher pseudo targets, which guarantee reliable student training, provide signal-level supervision via SI-SNR loss. Experiments on the real conversational evaluation set (EVAL-2) of the SLT 2026 REAL-TSE Challenge, the proposed method achieves a 6.53% relative TER reduction, together with relative improvements of 21.84% in speaker similarity, 9.89% in DNSMOS-P808, and 4.10% in target-activity F1 over the source-domain baseline, demonstrating its effectiveness under unseen real-world conditions.

Method Overview

RemixIT-TSE adopts a progressive synthetic-to-real adaptation strategy for real-world target speech extraction. Starting from a TSE model pretrained on synthetic data, Target-Aware Adaptation (TAA) first jointly exploits synthetic and weakly supervised real-world data to reduce the domain gap. RemixIT-TSE Adaptation (RTA) then further adapts the model using only real-world recordings through teacher-student remixing.

Synthetic only  →  Synthetic + Real  →  Real only

To adapt RemixIT to the TSE setting, two key modifications are introduced. First, quality-aware filtering retains reliable teacher pseudo-targets according to speaker consistency and transcription quality, reducing erroneous target-speaker supervision. Second, RemixIT-TSE removes direct supervision on the Non-target component, which may contain interfering speakers, noise, reverberation, and teacher errors, and instead optimizes the student only against the detached teacher Target using negative SI-SNR loss.

In this way, pseudo-target quality and target-speaker consistency are explicitly prioritized during real-world adaptation, while the Non-target component is used only for remixing.

Results

We evaluate RemixIT-TSE on the EVAL-1 and EVAL-2 sets of the SLT 2026 REAL-TSE Challenge. RemixIT-TSE consistently outperforms the source-domain SDP baseline under both seen and unseen real-world conditions.
